# VetCare – Reports Module ("Reports")

**Module:** 8 – Reports (Supporting)
**Sidebar location:** REPORTS → Reports
**Used by:** Administrator (full access)
**Tables:** No new tables. It only **reads** from Appointment, Owner, Pet, MedicalRecord, Vaccination, Medicine, Prescription, InventoryTransaction, Bill, BillItem, Payment, and User.

---

## 1. What this module does

The Reports module turns the clinic's saved data into **clear summaries** the Administrator can view, print, and export. It answers the fifth objective of the project: reports on appointments, medical records, medicine inventory, and financial transactions.

- Reports are grouped into **4 categories**: Appointments, Medical, Inventory, and Financial.
- Every report has **filters** (mainly a date range), **summary cards**, a **chart**, and a **table**.
- Reports can be **printed** or **exported** to PDF or Excel.

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| Administrator | View, print, and export all reports for their clinic |
| Veterinarian / Cashier/Staff | No access (they use their dashboards instead) |
| Super Admin | No access (clinic data is private) |

| # | Report | What it shows | Main tables |
|---|---|---|---|

| D4 | **Voided bills report** | Bills that were voided, by date and cashier | Bill, User |

### A. Reports home
- **Header** with the period selector shared by all reports (Today, This week, This month, Last month, Custom).
- **4 category sections** (Appointments, Medical, Inventory, Financial), each with **report cards**: icon, report name, a one-line description, and an **Open** button.

2. **Filter bar:** date range (required), plus filters that depend on the report (e.g., Vet, Status, Species, Payment method, Category). A **Generate** button.

5. **Table:** detailed rows, with sorting and a **Total** row at the bottom where it makes sense.

### C. Print / PDF layout
- A4, black-and-white friendly. It has the clinic name, address, and logo, the report name, the date range, the generated-by line, summary numbers, the chart, the table, and page numbers.

## 4. Rules (checked in ReportController)

1. Only the **Administrator** can open Reports.
2. All reports are **filtered by `Session.ClinicID`**, so a clinic only sees its own data.
3. Reports are **read-only**. They never change any data.
4. A **date range is required**, and the end date can't be before the start date. The longest range is **1 year**, to keep reports fast.
5. **Archived (soft-deleted) records are not counted.** **Voided bills** are left out of income and only appear in the Voided bills report.
6. **Collections vs. income:**
   - *Daily collection report* counts **money actually received** (Payment table).
   - *Income summary* counts **amounts billed** (BillItem table), whether paid or not.
7. **Rates** are calculated as: No-show rate = No-shows ÷ (Completed + No-shows) × 100.
8. **Stock value** = StockQuantity × UnitPrice. **Value lost** = quantity removed × UnitPrice.
9. Money is shown in **₱ with 2 decimals**. Dates use the clinic's local time (Asia/Manila).
10. Exports and printouts show **who generated** the report and **when**.

---

## 5. Offline / online sync

- Reports **can be generated offline**, using the data saved on this computer.
- When offline, or when changes are still pending, a warning banner shows: **"Offline — this report only includes data saved on this computer. Sync first for complete numbers."**
- The footer of every report shows the **last sync time**, so the admin knows how up to date it is.
- A **Sync now** button is available on the viewer for a complete report.
